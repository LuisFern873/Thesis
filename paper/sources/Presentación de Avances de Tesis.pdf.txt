Problem and Hypothesis
Problem
● Federated Learning (FL) struggles with data heterogeneity (non-IID data).
● Unknown how modern vision models behave under these conditions in medical imaging.
● Lack of benchmarks limits model selection for real deployments.
Hypothesis
● DeiT and Mamba will outperform CNNs under skewed FL. 
● Their global context modeling helps deal with client heterogeneity.
2
Motivation
Computational Motivation
● FL performance drops under non-IID (skewed) conditions. 
● Need to compare new vision architectures (DeiT, Mamba, ConvNeXt) vs CNNs in FL.
● Benchmarks help identify robustness to feature/label skew in medical images.
Practical Motivation
● Privacy laws (HIPAA/GDPR) prevent data sharing in healthcare.
● Medical data is naturally heterogeneous across institutions.
● Goal: Find models that stay reliable in real-world federated hospital settings.

2.4.
Federated methods
Federated Methods
Traditional FL Methods
● FedAvg: Baseline method; highlights performance drop under data heterogeneity. 
● FedProx: Adds regularization to handle client drift in non-IID data. 
● MOON: Uses contrastive learning to align representations and reduce heterogeneity impact.
FL Domain Generalization Methods
● FedSR: Enhances generalization by improving local feature representations.
● ADCOL: Uses contrastive learning to boost global and local consistency.
● FedIIR: Combines representation learning and reweighting to adapt to non-IID clients.

Key Observations
Model Performance Hierarchy
● EfﬁcientNetB0 demonstrates superior and most stable performance across both datasets
● DeiT-tiny shows strong performance but with higher variance
● Vim-tiny and ConvNeXt-tiny exhibit lower overall accuracy
Dataset-Speciﬁc Patterns
● IID Dataset: All models show steady convergence with EfﬁcientNetB0 reaching ~97% accuracy
● Non-IID Dataset: Higher volatility and instability, particularly affecting transformer-based models (DeiT-tiny, 
Vim-tiny)

Key Observations
Model Performance Ranking
● DeiT-tiny: Best overall performance, reaching ~78% accuracy with steady improvement
● ConvNeXt-tiny: Second best, ~70% accuracy with moderate stability
● EfﬁcientNetB0: Moderate performance (~65%) but shows catastrophic failure at round 16-17
● Vim-tiny: Consistently poorest performance, stagnating around 10-18%
Training Stability
● DeiT-tiny: Most stable with consistent upward trend
● ConvNeXt-tiny: Relatively stable with minor ﬂuctuations
● EfﬁcientNetB0: Dramatic collapse at round 16-17, recovering by round 18
● Vim-tiny: Persistently unstable and low-performing
